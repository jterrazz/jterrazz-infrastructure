# ADR-014: The registry is Zot, and collects its own garbage

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-24

Rewritten on 2026-10-24 when Zot replaced distribution. The distribution-era
record (a weekly `registry-gc` CronJob, the blob-descriptor cache turned off,
a boot panic on the read-only key) is in git history before commit 26ff18c.

## Context

The private registry (`registry.internal.jterrazz.com`, hostPath storage) is
the one every app deploy pushes to and every pod pulls from. Every CI push
moves `:latest` to a new manifest and leaves the old one behind, untagged but
holding its layers: on 2026-09-23 distribution held 796 manifests for 10 tags
and 14G of blobs.

distribution never deletes on its own. Collecting needed a CronJob running
`registry garbage-collect --delete-untagged`, which in turn needed the
in-memory blob-descriptor cache OFF (with it on, a swept layer stays "present"
and the next push re-using it writes an unpullable tag, distribution#1803), a
guard against collecting during an upload (read-only mode panicked 3.1.1 at
boot), a re-verified dry run before every image bump, and an alert for when
the job stopped. Four workarounds for one missing feature.

Since 2026-10-24 every app deploys **by digest** (studio-actions
docker-deploy), so a running workload references a manifest that may no longer
carry a tag.

## Decision

The registry is **Zot** (`ghcr.io/project-zot/zot`), an OCI-native registry
whose garbage collection and retention are built in, configured in
`src/kubernetes/services/registry/app.yaml`:

- `gc` every 6h with a 1h delay; `retention` removes untagged manifests and
  their referrers (attestations, SBOMs) 24h after they lose their tag.
- `compat: ["docker2s2"]` keeps Docker-format manifests byte-identical, so a
  digest never changes on its way in.
- `dedupe` on: the same layer pushed by two apps is stored once.
- Auth stays htpasswd, Secret `registry-auth`, generated by
  `roles/releases/tasks/main.yml` from the `studio-ci` Infisical project,
  now with two accounts: the push account (CI), whose rotation still overlaps
  two names ([Operate](../guides/operate.md#rotating-the-registry-password)),
  and `kubelet`, which `accessControl` limits to `read` and from which every
  namespace's pull secret is built. Anonymous gets nothing.
- Probes are Zot's own `/readyz` and `/livez` (`/v2/` answers 401).

The move was a seeded swap: a throwaway Zot on the node, bound to 127.0.0.1,
filled the new volume from distribution with `regctl image copy` (7
repositories, 42 tags, 0 failures, every digest a running pod used present,
digests preserved), then the `registry` release was upgraded in place. 1.2G
against distribution's 13G.

## Consequences

- No CronJob, no alert, no cache flag, no dry-run ritual before a bump.
- A digest that loses its tag lives 24h, then goes. Every deploy moves all of
  an app's environments to the new digest in one run, so this only bites if a
  deploy fails between two environments and a pod of the stale one restarts a
  day later with the image gone from the node's cache: redeploy the app.
- distribution's store and its PV were deleted on 2026-10-25, a day after the
  move: there is no rollback to distribution.
- A leaked pull secret (any app namespace's `registry-credentials`) can read
  images, never push or delete one.
